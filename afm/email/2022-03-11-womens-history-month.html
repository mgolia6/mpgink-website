Women's History Month.

FRIDAY 3/11/2022
AFM | Women's History Month | 3/11/2022
From the email era. This went out as a plain email — not on LinkedIn — to a work distribution list. Signature blocks, phone numbers and work links have been removed, and colleagues are referred to by role rather than by name. The writing is unchanged.

Hau’oli Aloha Po’alima (Happy Aloha Friday),

Yesterday marked 99 years since the death of Harriet Tubman. Tubman, most famous for her work on the underground railroad, working to free over 70 slaves in her lifetime, risking certain death if caught, was a true American hero who stood up for what she believed was right. I urge you to find some time to review these Amazing Facts about her life. Her quote below, is so believable, so impactful, because, in the face of true adversity, she did just that…changed the world.

I also want to highlight another true American hero, Autherine Lucy Foster, who died last week, at the age of 92. 10 years before the Stand in the Schoolhouse Door, Autherine, after completing her bachelor’s degree at Miles College in Alabama, applied to the University of Alabama to obtain a second degree, and was subsequently accepted by the university…until they realized she was black. Read all about her story here.

To add a punch of music to this beautiful day, let’s celebrate Bobby McFerrin, who was born on this day in 1950, and his instant classic Don't Worry Be Happy!

Mahalo Nui!
