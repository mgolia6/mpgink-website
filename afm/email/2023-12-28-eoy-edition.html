Happy Aloha Friday!

Another year is in the record books. So I thought I would create a new tradition, cause I had so much fun with last year’s final post, and just use the same post. I did notice big miss on my part, sharing a Phish New Year’s Tradition, so I added that…and just like that giant ball in times square, what goes up, must come down…right!?
