Happy Aloha Friday!

Inspiration strikes more often than you think. The question is: are you actually paying attention as it moves the speed of light [across the universe](https://youtu.be/90M60PzmxEE) and zaps you…or are you too busy to take notice. Its easy to get caught up in the rigamarole. For me though, June and Pride month is reset, a grounding, one of those months that inspires me [from such great heights](https://youtu.be/gQLvhLbs3X4). The [Origins of Pride Month](https://www.history.com/topics/gay-rights/pride-month) stem back to the early 1900s but it was the [June 28th 1969 raid of the Stonewall Inn](https://www.history.com/news/stonewall-riots-timeline) in Greenwich Village that sparked six days of protests that moved the gay rights movement from the periphery to global news.

For me, the inspiration stems from the bravery of those in the LGBTQ+ community to be their authentic selves in spite of great adversity. Though you may not directly identify with the community, all of humanity can identify with the struggle to find one’s voice and be openly and unabashedly “themselves” throughout their life. Society does not always make that easy for minority groups. And that is certainly true of the LGBTQ+ community even in these “modern” times. Ask yourself one more question on this Friday: What inspiration is tugging at your pant leg right now, like some persistent child, that you might be shaking away?
