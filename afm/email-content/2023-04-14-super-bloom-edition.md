Happy Aloha Friday!It was Jean Paul Sartre who inspired my philosophy degree 23 years ago in Arnold Hall on the Hartwick College campus during my Philosophy in Literature course with Professor Stanley Konecky. Something about that long table, all the wood of that room, the discussion style setting, students circled around it, trying to make heads and tales of his magnum opus Being and Nothingness. Then we read The Stranger and was inspired to write, to think critically, to THINK (maybe too much sometimes, my wife would say)!? Enjoy these [Jean Paul Sartre | Fun Facts](https://blog.bookstellyouwhy.com/five-interesting-facts-about-jean-paul-sartre).
