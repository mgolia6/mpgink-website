Every year as Thanksgiving approaches, time honored traditions are followed, new ones are forged, and memoires are forever made. My favorite time honored tradition for Thanksgiving is searching across the FM stations, traveling from family house to family house in the car, feverishly listening for Arlo Guthrie’s [Alice's Restaurant](https://youtu.be/m57gzA2JCcM). Historically, radio stations, on Thanksgiving, have played this Opus in its entirety due to its reference to Thanksgiving. It is an 18 minute masterpiece of musical parody protest poetry and worth a listen.
