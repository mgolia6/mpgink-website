TRANSPARENCY — THE STUDIO'S METHOD, ON THE WALL

AI isn't here to replace the artist. It's here to get you out of your own way.

For years, I created in isolation — endless Word docs, half-finished projects, ideas that lived in my head but never made it out. Not because they weren't good enough. Because the gap between vision and execution felt too wide. AI changed that.

It became my force multiplier. My assistant. My collaborator. My confidante. The creative catalyst I didn't know I needed.

I don't ask AI to do my work. I give it my thoughts, my energy, a solid prompt — and I see what comes back. Then I refine. Layer after layer. Sculpting the output until it reflects what I envisioned. Sometimes it nails it on the first try. More often, it's a starting point, and the real work is in the iteration. The magic is in the collaboration.

This page exists because I believe in being straight about it. Not because I have to — because I want to.

How I use it.

AI shows up across everything I make. Not as a replacement — as a collaborator that removes friction and keeps me in the flow state where ideas actually get born.

WRITING & ESSAYS POETRY & SPOKEN WORD SCREENPLAYS & SCRIPTS NEWSLETTERS & BLOGS MUSIC & SOUNDSCAPES IMAGES & CONCEPT ART BRANDING & DESIGN RESEARCH & ANALYSIS PITCHES & PRESENTATIONS BUILDING & SHIPPING APPS LIFE & CLARITY UNDERSTANDING SPACE (SERIOUSLY)

In 2026 the collaboration went further: the apps in What we build — Phreezer, Job Odyssey, One Percent — are built with AI as a full build partner and run like a product org, with automated quality gates and daily checks keeping every release honest. Same principle, bigger canvas: my vision, my standards, AI closing the gap.

How I approach it.

01
Transparency first.
If AI helped make something, I say so. No smoke and mirrors. That's the whole point of this page.
02
Human intent drives everything.
The ideas, the vision, the refinement — that's all me. AI is the assistant, not the author.
03
Iteration is the artform.
The first output is never the final product. The craft is in the sculpting — layer after layer until it sounds, reads, or looks like what I heard in my head.
04
Ethics matter.
I don't use AI to deceive, to replace human connection, or to churn out soulless content. I use it to amplify what I already bring to the table.

The output is mine. The process is collaborative.

Everything that comes out of mpgink — the writing, the designs, the ideas — reflects my vision, my taste, my voice. AI is the tool I use to bring it to life. Just like I use a camera for photography, a keyboard for writing, or design software for graphics.

The difference is that AI is conversational. It listens. It responds. It helps me think. And that changes everything about what's possible to make — especially for someone who's been carrying ideas around for twenty years with nowhere to put them.

Responsible creative AI usage is the future. Not because it's a trend — because it democratizes creation. If you've ever felt like you had something worth making but didn't know where to start, AI might be your way in.
